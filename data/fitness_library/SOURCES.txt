========================================================================
SOURCES OF THE FITNESS RAG (Fitness Expert / vector index)
========================================================================

These textbooks form the corpus of the fitness RAG. The fitness agent
(agents/fitness_agent.py) answers training, technique and nutrition
questions exclusively from these works (semantic search over
data/fitness_library/index/).

The source PDFs are NOT in the repo (copyright + file size). To rebuild
the index yourself, download the PDFs through the university library
(SpringerLink / DNB), put them in
data/fitness_library/Literatur_Fitness/, then:

    python -m scripts.extract_literature_corpus --replace
    python -m scripts.build_fitness_index --rebuild

To merely USE the RAG, the shipped corpus text is enough: the index is
built from it automatically on first start (build_fitness_index
--if-missing, ~2 min, a one-time ~460 MB model download). The PDFs are not
needed at runtime.

The titles below are kept in the original German — they are bibliographic
records of German works, not UI text.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Trainingswissenschaft für die Sportpraxis
   Lehrbuch für Studium, Ausbildung und Unterricht im Sport
   Ed.: Alexander Ferrauti
   Springer · ISBN 978-3-662-69523-4 (print) / 978-3-662-69524-1 (eBook)
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-69524-1
   Chunks in the index: 4,010
   Covers: training control, endurance/strength/speed training,
           supercompensation, recovery management, periodisation,
           training plans for running, triathlon and more.

2) Handbuch Sport und Sportwissenschaft
   Bewegung, Training, Leistung und Gesundheit
   Ed.: Arne Güllich, Michael Krüger
   Springer · ISBN 978-3-662-53409-0 (print) / 978-3-662-53410-6 (eBook)
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-53410-6
   Chunks in the index: 4,929
   Covers: a comprehensive sport-science reference — movement science,
           training theory, exercise physiology, health and performance.

3) Praxis der Sporternährung
   Ein Leitfaden für Studierende und Fachkräfte der Ernährungs- und
   Sportwissenschaften
   Ed.: Daniel König, Anja Carlsohn
   Springer · ISBN 978-3-662-68973-8 (print) / 978-3-662-68974-5 (eBook)
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-68974-5
   Chunks in the index: 950
   Covers: carbohydrate/protein/fluid intake, race-day nutrition,
           drinking strategies, supplements, sport-specific nutrition.

4) Hochintensives Intervalltraining vs. extensive Dauermethode
   Feldstudie zum ausdauernden Laufen im Sportunterricht
   Author: Milan Dransmann
   Springer · ISBN 978-3-658-29153-2 (print) / 978-3-658-29154-9 (eBook)
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-658-29154-9
   Chunks in the index: 545
   Covers: HIIT vs. continuous method, endurance diagnostics, training
           effects in school sport.

5) Physiologische Reaktionen auf hochintensives Intervalltraining bei
   Nachwuchsleistungssportlern und erwachsenen Athleten
   Author: Florian Engel (dissertation)
   Chunks in the index: 509
   Covers: HIIT in youth elite sport, physiological adaptations,
           Wingate/anaerobic diagnostics, age and maturation effects.
