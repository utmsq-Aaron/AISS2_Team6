VERIFICATION STATUS: CONFIRMED
SOURCE CONFIRMS: The PDF is John Brooke's original book chapter "SUS - A quick and dirty usability scale" (in: Jordan, Thomas, Weerdmeester, McClelland (eds.), Usability Evaluation in Industry, Taylor & Francis, 1996). It defines the SUS as a ten-item Likert questionnaire yielding a single composite score in the range 0-100, intended as a reliable, low-cost instrument for global usability assessment after a user has worked with a system. This is exactly how our usability-study section uses it: administered after the three predefined tasks, reported as a 0-100 score in Table tab:usability.
